an unforgettable evening

A prom night, made for you.

Night to Shine is a complimentary event for people with special needs, hosted by local churches around the world. The event is open to anyone living with disabilities, ages 14 and older.

Each event is unique to its location, but every one of them celebrates each guest the way God sees them, every single day.

what to expect

What Night to Shine is known for.

Every Night to Shine is unique to its location. Here are some of the things guests can look forward to at events like this one:

🎬Red carpet entrance & paparazzi
💄Hair & makeup stations
👞Shoeshines
🚘Limousine rides
🎤Karaoke
🎁Gifts
🍽️Catered dinner
🌈Sensory Room
☕Respite Room for parents & caregivers
💃Dancing

And the grand finale: a crowning ceremony where every guest is honored as a King or a Queen.

Where everyone is royalty!
